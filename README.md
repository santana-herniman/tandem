# Tandem

> Know what you're signing.

Tandem is an AI-powered tool that reads terms and conditions, privacy policies, and legal agreements — and explains them in plain English. Red flag alerts, fairness scores, and instant answers to your questions. In seconds.

## The problem

91% of people never read terms and conditions. Companies count on that. Auto-renewals, data sharing clauses, arbitration waivers — they're buried on page 9 in size 10 font. Tandem puts you back in control.

## What Tandem does

- **Plain language summary** — what the document actually means for you
- **Red flag detection** — auto-renewals, data sharing, liability waivers surfaced automatically
- **Fairness score** — every document rated out of 10 for how consumer-friendly it is
- **Ask anything** — get direct answers to specific questions about any document
- **Scan history** — every document you've decoded, saved and searchable
